What Affects the Price?

Two cars never cost exactly the same to detail. These are the five things that move the price:

1. Vehicle size

A compact sedan takes less time and product than a 3-row SUV or a full-size truck. Bigger vehicle, bigger price — usually by $20–$50.

2. Condition

A car detailed every month is quick. A car that has not been cleaned in a year — with stains, ground-in dirt, or sticky residue — takes much longer.

3. Interior vs. exterior

Interior-only work (shampoo, stain treatment, pet hair) and exterior-only work (wash, wax, decontamination) cost less than a full inside-and-out detail.

4. Add-ons

Pet hair removal, odor removal, engine bay cleaning, and headlight restoration are add-ons at $30–$60 each. You only pay for what you need.

The fifth factor is paint condition. Swirl marks, oxidation, and water spots need paint correction before any coating — that is skilled machine work, priced separately after we see the paint.

Why Houston Cars Cost What They Cost

Houston is hard on cars. Summer heat and UV fade paint and crack dashboards. Humidity feeds mildew and odors in the interior. Construction dust, pollen, and highway grime build up fast. That is why a quick car wash never really lasts here — and why a proper detail, done right, is worth more than it costs.

A full detail in Houston is not a luxury. It is maintenance: it protects your paint from the sun, keeps your interior from smelling like a gym bag in August, and keeps your resale value up when it is time to sell.

Mobile Detailing vs. Car Wash

A drive-through car wash costs $10–$20 and takes ten minutes. It also misses the interior entirely, leaves swirl marks, and does nothing about stains, smells, or protection. Mobile detailing is a different service: trained hands, professional products, 2–4 hours of work, at your location — and your car comes back genuinely clean, inside and out.

If you just need dust off the paint, a car wash is fine. If you want your car to look and smell like it just left the showroom, that is detailing. See what a full interior detail includes, or what ceramic coating does for Houston sun protection.
