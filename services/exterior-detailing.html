FAQs

Will an exterior detail scratch my paint?

No. We use a foam pre-soak, grit-guard buckets, and clean microfiber mitts to prevent swirls. It is far safer than automatic car washes.

Do you offer Exterior Detailing in the Houston area?

Yes. We serve the Houston area and surrounding neighborhoods near Downtown Houston and the Galleria. We bring our own water and power to your location.

How often should I get an exterior detail?

Every 2 to 4 weeks keeps paint in great shape in the Houston climate. Ask about our maintenance wash plans for a recurring schedule.
