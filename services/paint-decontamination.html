Paint Decontamination — Every Houston Area

Even a freshly washed car can feel rough — that is bonded contamination: iron particles, tar, tree sap, and industrial fallout stuck in the clear coat. Our Paint Decontamination uses iron removers and clay-bar treatment to pull it all out, leaving paint glass-smooth and ready for wax, sealant, or coating.

FAQs

How do I know if my paint needs decontamination?

Run your hand over the paint after washing. If it feels rough or gritty instead of smooth, it has bonded contamination.

Do you offer paint decontamination in the Houston area?

Yes. We serve the Houston area mobile, including areas near Downtown Houston and the Galleria.

Is decontamination included in a ceramic coating?

Yes — full decontamination is always part of our ceramic coating prep. As a standalone, it is the perfect prep before any wax or sealant.
