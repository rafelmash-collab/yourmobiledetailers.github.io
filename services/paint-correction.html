FAQs

What is the difference between paint correction and waxing?

Waxing hides imperfections temporarily. Paint correction permanently removes swirls and defects by leveling the clear coat with machine polishing.

Do you offer paint correction in the Houston area?

Yes. We perform mobile paint correction across the Houston area, from Downtown Houston to the Galleria. The vehicle should be in a shaded area for the best results.

Will paint correction remove deep scratches?

It permanently removes swirls, haze, and light scratches. Deeper scratches are greatly minimized. We inspect the paint first and tell you honestly what to expect.
