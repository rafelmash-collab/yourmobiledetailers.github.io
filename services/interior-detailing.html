FAQs

How long does Interior Detailing take?

Most interior details take 2 to 3 hours. Heavily soiled vehicles or third-row SUVs can take a little longer, and we will confirm timing when you book.

Do you offer Interior Detailing in the Houston area?

Yes. Our mobile unit serves the Houston area daily, from Downtown Houston to the Galleria. We detail right in your driveway — no need to visit a shop.

Can you remove stains from seats and carpets?

Yes. Most food, drink, and everyday stains lift with our hot-water extraction process. Older set-in stains are significantly improved, and we always set honest expectations before we start.
