FAQs

Does steam cleaning really sanitize?

Yes. Steam above 200°F kills most bacteria, dust mites, and allergens on contact — without harsh chemicals.

Do you offer steam cleaning in the Houston area?

Yes. We serve the Houston area mobile, from Downtown Houston to the Galleria.

Is steam safe for my car's electronics?

Yes. We use controlled vapor and avoid direct steam on sensitive electronics. It is safer than soaking surfaces with liquid cleaners.
