FAQs

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Our coatings last 2 to 5 years depending on the package, maintenance, and how the vehicle is stored. We will recommend the right level for how you use your car.

Can you apply ceramic coating at my home in the Houston area?

Yes. We apply coatings mobile across the Houston area, including areas near Downtown Houston and the Galleria. The vehicle needs a shaded or covered spot for application and curing.

What affects the price of ceramic coating?

Vehicle size, paint condition, and how much correction is needed before coating. We inspect the paint first and give you an exact quote — starting at $399.
