With our UV sealant, clarity typically lasts 1 to 2 years. Without sealant, haze returns in months — which is why sealing is always included.

Yes. We restore headlights mobile across the Houston area, from Downtown Houston to the Galleria. Most jobs take under an hour.

For cloudy lenses, yes — restoration costs a fraction of replacement and restores both appearance and light output.
