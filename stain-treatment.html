FAQs

Can all stains be removed?

Most fresh stains come out completely. Older, set-in, or dye-transfer stains are significantly improved. We assess each stain honestly before starting.

Do you offer stain treatment in your area?

Yes. We serve your area mobile, from your home to your office.

Should I try to clean the stain myself first?

Blot — never rub — and avoid heat. Home remedies can set stains; call us first for the best chance of full removal: (323) 880-4067.
