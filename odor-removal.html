FAQs

Can you remove cigarette smoke smell?

Yes. Smoke is one of the most common odors we treat. Enzyme cleaning plus ozone treatment neutralizes it from upholstery, headliner, and vents.

Do you offer odor removal in your area?

Yes. We serve your area mobile, including areas near your home and your office. Ozone treatment is done on-site with the vehicle sealed.

How long does the treatment take?

Plan on 2 to 3 hours including the ozone cycle. Severe odors may need a second pass, which we will quote honestly up front.
